# HR-Analytics-Dashboard
## Project Overview
### This project presents an interactive HR Analytics dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI. The dashboard provides insights into estimated annual financial cost of employee attrition to the company and which departments, job roles, cost components and salary bands driving the highest cost.
## Project Objective
- Estimate the annual financial cost of employee attrition to the company.
- Identify which departments, job roles, and salary bands drive the highest attrition cost.
- Perform exploratory data analysis (EDA) across demographics, tenure, compensation, engagement, and performance to uncover attrition patterns.
- Compare leavers vs. stayers across satisfaction, work-life balance, engagement, and manager effectiveness.
- Deliver an interactive Power BI dashboard for stakeholder exploration.

## Exploratory Data Analysis
Analyzed 6,500 employee records using SQL to answer 25+ business questions across the following areas:
- Attrition Overview- Attrition cost by department, job-level, and salary(monthly income) band.
- Cost breakdown components - (recruitment, training, productivity loss, exit, onboarding).
- Tenure & Career Growth - tenure and time-in-role comparison (leavers vs. stayers), impact of promotions and internal mobility on attrition.
- Compensation and Benefits - income, bonus%, and benefits satisfaction comparison (leavers vs. stayers), impact of internal mobility and promotion on attrition.
- Engagement & Satisfaction - job-satisfaction, work-life balance, engagement score comparison (leavers vs. stayers).
- Performance & Workload- Impact on attrition by performance, workload, absenteeism.
- Demographics Summary- Attrition pattern by age group, gender, education level.

## Business Value
This dashboard can support HR Teams and leadership because:
- Quantifies a hidden cost by tracking actual cost driven by attrition.
- Prioritizes retention spend by showing which departments/job levels/salary bands drive the highest cost (not just highest headcount loss), leadership can target retention budgets where they'll save the most money, not just where the most people are leaving.
